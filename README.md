Description: Having made a mistake in your lifetime, you are now but a remnant of your past - the only thing resembling your previous form is your personality.
Performance impact: 🪽 🪽

Strengths / Powers:
Acrobatics: You never take fall damage, no matter from which height you fall.
Ascension: Levitate up into the air on command (Toggle with Primary Key).
Non-Living Body: Being a revived form, Wither and Poison have no effect on you.
Ally of the Undead: Undead mobs recognize you as one of their own due to your revived form, and will not attack you.

Weaknesses / Limits: 
Restless Mind: You are unable to sleep due to the weight of your past mistakes.
Carnivore: Revival has made you meat-hungry, you can only properly digest and chew up meats.
Fragile Form: Revival has left your body weaker, leaving you with one less heart than others.
